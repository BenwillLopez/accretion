Privacy

What Accretion keeps, why, and how to remove it.

Browsing without an account

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your saved apps and last-visit date stay in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.

If you create an account

Accounts run on Supabase, which stores:

  • your email address and a securely hashed password, used only to sign you in and for sign-in or reset emails;
  • your username, display name, bio and avatar color, which are public;
  • your posts, comments, likes, ratings and follows, which are public;
  • reports you file, which only moderators can see;
  • your notifications, which only you can see.

If you sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub or Discord, that service tells Accretion your email address, your name, and your handle where it has one. These pre-fill your profile, and you can change them. With Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", and Accretion then only sees a private relay address. Accretion never receives your password for those services and can't post on them.

Your email is never shown to other users or moderators. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Deleting your account

Signed in, open your profile, tap Edit profile, then Delete account. Your account, profile, posts, likes, ratings and follows are deleted right away.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub, or send a private report through GitHub security advisories.
