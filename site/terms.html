The short version: Accretion is free, comes with no guarantees, and you decide what you install.

What Accretion is

Accretion is a free store and iOS app that lists apps published in public AltStore and SideStore sources, plus apps and mods Accretion builds itself. Apps from other sources are made and hosted by other people. Accretion doesn't review them and isn't responsible for what they do. Install only what you trust.

Your account

You need an account only for community features and to back up certificates. Keep your sign-in safe, and follow the community rules. Accretion may hide posts, remove accounts or block access when the rules are broken. You can delete your account at any time from your profile.

Ads and support

The iOS app shows a small ad while apps download; supporters don't see ads. Some links are affiliate links, and Accretion may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See the privacy page for what ads and accounts collect.
