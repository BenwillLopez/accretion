Community rules

Short version: talk about apps, help each other, be decent.

Who can join

You need to be 13 or older to make an account.

Keep it useful

  • Share how an app works for you: device, iOS version, bugs, fixes, tips.
  • Ask and answer questions. Say thanks when someone helps.
  • Report broken apps or dead links so they get fixed.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate speech or slurs.
  • Spam, ads, referral links, or posting the same thing over and over.
  • Links to malware, phishing, fake installers or anything that steals accounts.
  • Other people's personal information.
  • Sexual content, gore, or anything illegal.
  • Pretending to be someone else, including staff of other projects.

Moderation

Anyone signed in can report a post or a profile. Moderators review reports and can hide posts or ban accounts. Hidden posts stay visible to their author. Posting is rate-limited to stop spam.

Apps

Accretion lists apps from public third-party sources and does not review them. Comments and ratings are opinions from other users, not endorsements. Install only what you trust.
